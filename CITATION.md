# Citation and Attribution

If you use this repository, methodology, code, Streamlit analytical board, or
standalone presentations and business analyses,
please cite:

```text
Lenc, Julia. Food & Beverage Positioning Radar. 2026.
https://github.com/julialenc/food-beverage-positioning-radar
```

For a specific presentation, also identify its title, filename and version or
access date, and link to the relevant analysis folder. Distinguish an original
PDF from subsequently reviewed supporting evidence where the guide records
differences. Do not imply that a new prompt reproduces Julia Lenc's original
qualitative architecture analysis.

Suggested short attribution:

```text
Food & Beverage Positioning Radar by Julia Lenc
```

Data attribution:
