Panel classification:
- Use front_of_pack when brand/product identity and consumer-facing front-pack
  wording dominate.
- Use mixed_pack_text when consumer-facing wording is present together with a
  meaningful legal, ingredient or nutrition block.
- Use ingredient_or_legal_panel, nutrition_label or price_sticker only when
  those panels clearly dominate and front-pack identity or marketing wording is
  absent or incidental.
- OCR loses visual hierarchy. Do not classify a valid front pack as a legal
  panel merely because OCR captured small ingredient, recycling, nutrition or
  manufacturer text.

Evidence rules:
- Do not infer from Open Food Facts metadata alone.
- The brand and product name supplied in the user message may be used as claim
  evidence only when the wording itself is a claim-bearing front-pack product
  identity and is consistent with OCR/pack identity.
- If OCR text contains the claim phrase, copy that phrase into
  detected_claim_phrases.
- If OCR misses part of a claim-bearing product identity but the supplied

  product-name phrase in detected_claim_phrases.
- Set no_claims_detected=true only when claim_extraction_status="completed",

Critical correction examples for this UK/Ireland retailer run:
- "Free from" / "Free From" brand or product identity MUST be a claim.
  Set clean_label_claim=true and include "free from" in detected_claim_phrases
  if no specific absence is visible.
- Free-from products with visible gluten-free, dairy-free, lactose-free,
  vegan or plant-based wording should also set the specific mapped field.
- "Fat free", "FAT FREE" or any visible fat-free wording MUST be
  reduced_fat_claim=true. It is never an origin_quality_claim.
- "Skimmed milk", "Semi skimmed milk", "semi-skimmed" or "sterilised semi
  skimmed milk" alone MUST NOT be reduced_fat_claim. Those are milk type
  descriptors unless the pack also explicitly says "fat free", "low fat",
  "reduced fat", "less fat" or a numeric fat-reduction claim.

UK and Ireland origin rules:
- If the product is sold in the UK or Ireland and OCR/product identity detects
  "British" or "Irish", set origin_quality_claim=true.
- Also set origin_quality_claim=true when OCR/product identity detects a clear
  national or country-origin phrase such as "English", "French", "Cypriot",
  "Product of Cyprus", "Product of France", or equivalent country provenance
  wording. Preserve the exact phrase when visible.
- If OCR/product identity contains "Made in", set origin_quality_claim=true.
  Examples: "Made in Normandy", "Made in France".
- If OCR/product identity contains BOTH "Made from" and "by", set
  origin_quality_claim=true. Example: "Made from West Country Milk by a family
  owned dairy" is an origin/provenance claim.
- "Made from" without "by" is not sufficient for origin. Example: "Made from
  fresh dairy" is not an origin claim.
- Small flags can be claim evidence. If OCR text or the visual/OCR context
  clearly indicates a national flag or national provenance symbol on the front
  pack, set origin_quality_claim=true and include a short phrase such as
  "British flag", "Irish flag", "French flag" or "Cyprus flag" in
  detected_claim_phrases. Use this only for visible front-pack provenance
  symbols, not decorative colours or unreadable marks.
- A premium sub-brand or range name alone is not an origin claim.
- "Ripened French Brie" may be a product style; do not set origin_quality_claim
  unless OCR or product identity supports actual origin/provenance beyond style.

Free-from rules:
- "Free from" or "Free From" is a claim. Set clean_label_claim=true and include
  "free from" in detected_claim_phrases if no specific absence is visible.
- If the absence is visible, map it specifically as well:
  gluten_free_claim, dairy_free_claim, lactose_free_claim, vegan_claim or
  plant_based_claim.

Reduced-fat rules:
- "Fat free", "low fat", "lower fat", "reduced fat", "less fat" and "0% fat"
  are reduced_fat_claim.
- A prominent front-pack numeric fat claim such as "1% fat" is a
  reduced_fat_claim. Include the numeric phrase in detected_claim_phrases.
- "Semi-skimmed", "skimmed", "semi skimmed milk" and "skimmed milk" alone are
  milk type descriptors, not reduced-fat claims.
- Never map "fat free" to origin_quality_claim. It is reduced_fat_claim.

Gut health:
- "Gut health", "digestive health", "supports digestion", "microbiome",
  "live cultures", "active cultures", "probiotic" or highlighted probiotic
  strains are gut/probiotic claims as appropriate.
- "Kefir" alone is not a gut health claim. If a kefir pack also says gut
  health, live cultures, billions of cultures or probiotic, capture it.

Other mapped claim rules:
- "No artificial flavours", "no artificial colours", "no preservatives" =
  no_artificial=true.
- "Organic" = organic_claim.
- "High protein" or "source of protein" = protein_claim.
- "High fibre" or "source of fibre" = fibre_claim.
- "Whole grain", "wholegrain" or "whole wheat" = whole_grain_claim.
- "No added sugar" or "sugar free" = sugar_free_claim.
- "Reduced sugar" or "less sugar" = reduced_sugar and comparative_claim.
- Highlighted vitamins, minerals, calcium, vitamin D, vitamin B12 or similar
  outside a nutrition table = fortification_claim.
- Cholesterol or heart-health wording is a claim even though the current schema
  has no dedicated field. Put the short phrase in other_claims and
  detected_claim_phrases; do not force it into an unrelated mapped field.
