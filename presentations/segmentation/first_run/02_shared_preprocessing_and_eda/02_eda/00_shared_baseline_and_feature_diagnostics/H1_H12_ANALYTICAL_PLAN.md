## Common population and measurement rules

Use the corrected 17 October 2026 retrieval with the 29 September reporting cutoff. Start from 376,845 product-scope-eligible barcode–region–category assignments. Keep barcode as text. The master supplies the key, product scope, working nutrients, OFF source observations, missing flags, locked claims and ingredient evidence. The existing classification supplies membership, subtype, confidence, cereal bar flag and comparison basis. Join all three key fields exactly once. The full outer join reconciled every assignment. The shared implementation verifies the source hash and the baseline valid counts for all eight nutrients in each region and category. Corrected master manifest: `first_run/01_ingestion/05_master_parquet/output/manifest.json` (local frozen input) · [Baseline EDA](CHECK.md#baseline-eda) · [Shared diagnostic script](shared_feature_diagnostics_v1.py)

**Peer** means membership YES, a named subtype, a comparable food or liquid basis and no existing cereal bar-review flag. There are 292,302 such assignments. Analyse within region × category × subtype × basis unless a row explicitly specifies pooling. Keep OTHER, unresolved membership, unresolved subtype, possible category contamination, preparation and unverified basis in the governed population and its coverage denominators. Use the existing food per 100 g and liquid per 100 ml conventions, including liquid dairy. Pack-level units are not independently verified. Preparations may contribute format and communication evidence as a separate stratum. Never convert them to ready-to-drink equivalents. Repeat supported contrasts without LOW-confidence assignments as a classification sensitivity, keeping original unrated assignments identifiable.

**Valid** means the baseline working value is present, matches the OFF observation, has a clear missing flag and passes its finite governed range. Keep observed zero. There is no imputation. Energy is kcal and the seven other measurements are grams per applicable 100-unit basis. Core four means protein, total fat, total sugars and salt. All four are existing measurements. Densities, component shares, ranks, match indicators, quartile flags and ingredient cues are analytical derivations. The four numeric ratios and the ingredient cue were constructed provisionally in memory for the diagnostics. The other analytical features described below would be constructed after approval. Raw fields, formulas and retention: `FEATURE_DECISIONS.csv` (local working output)

**Claim comparison** means a matched, analytically eligible assessment of the exact proposition, with no source-market limitation for the regional contrasts. Compare a locked positive against documented non-detection. Keep human-confirmed negatives separate for sensitivity. Keep uncertain, unassessed, unmatched and inapplicable observations distinguishable in the source and outside that contrast. Report original core states and evidence cohort. Use the derived energy-positioning indicator and independent usable front hydration and electrolyte assessments with their own denominators. Exclude a salt-specific QA hold only when using salt. Identify historical dairy reuse and the US dairy population note in sensitivity results. Ingredient eligibility is independent of nutrition eligibility.

**Evidence rules proposed for the test run.** Publish starting, measurable, assessed and retained counts by cell; report percentages with explicit denominators. Use France and UK/Ireland as primary replication opportunities, and US/Canada as selected exploratory observations. For nutritional estimates require at least 30 valid records per cell; for claim contrasts require at least 20 positives and 20 documented non-detections in the same cell before matching. Recheck sizes after matching. These are reporting floors, not power calculations. Keep smaller cells descriptive. Use barcode-level block bootstrap 95% intervals for effects and proportions, preserving repeated regional assignments. Where multiple prespecified contrasts are tested, report Benjamini–Hochberg adjusted p-values within each hypothesis alongside practical effects. Lack of support, imprecision and inadequate sample size are separate outcomes. No causal, compliance, sales or consumer-prevalence interpretation.

## Feature and implementation decisions

- **Protein claim provenance.** Of 385 H5 protein positives with comparable measured protein, 191 have direct OCR wording, 169 a saved model assessment, 24 an approved existing rule and one a historical saved assessment. Compare the primary estimate with a direct-wording estimate. Report rule-supported positives separately because an existing numeric protein implication can contribute to the classification. The locked proposition records communicated positioning; the nutritional outcome comes from the separate OFF measurement.
- **Shared nutrition basis.** Retain total fat and sugars in the core matrix. Saturated fat remains a meaningful absolute measure for targeted profiling. Total carbohydrates supplies the context for sugars. Adding both parents and components to a distance would give some nutritional axes extra weight. The pooled food reference gives fat/saturated-fat Pearson 0.871 and Spearman 0.845, and carbohydrates/sugars 0.736 and 0.762. Relationships differ by format. Interpret correlations on their printed paired counts. Energy is a matching or denominator variable where the question requires it. The all-eight matrix has energy VIF 118, fat 99 and carbohydrates 67. The four core VIFs range from 1.09 to 1.44. [Correlation evidence and regeneration](CHECK.md#correlation-evidence)
- **Ratios.** Missing numerator, missing denominator and observed zero denominator yield undefined ratios. A measured zero numerator with a valid positive denominator yields zero. Keep primary density rankings at ≥10 kcal per 100 units and show alternatives. Review tail leverage and rank displacement before interpreting an extreme. Use component shares as secondary descriptors only; flag values above one and parent amounts below 1 g, report sensitivities restricted to coherent pairs and parents ≥1 g. Do not cap, repair or delete the source. Ratio extremes can reflect rounding, small denominators or discordant inputs; diagnostics cannot identify the cause for an individual product.
- **Scaling and ties.** Report nutritional contrasts in original units. Use peer ranks for H3/H4 and within-cell quartiles for specific comparisons. For high/low H1 use strict above Q3/below Q1; flag cells where either indicator is empty. For H4 report tie counts and use inclusive concentration and energy quartiles only when boundaries distinguish groups. A strict H9/H10 threshold excludes ties exactly at Q3. Reserve robust IQR calipers for H6 and reference-percentile scaling for the one control. Percentiles bound numerical leverage but discard absolute distance, so report original-unit medians/IQRs and tails for every control group. No global log transform, outlier filter, normalization or imputation.
- **Execution and interpretation.** Group resamples by barcode and preserve the analysis unit in uncertainty estimates; for H2/H6 resample whole matched pairs within a cell. For H12 use a shared-barcode sensitivity in addition to block resampling. Recalculate study-specific peer thresholds within resamples when population-estimated. Lock pairing tolerances, tails, reporting floors and practical thresholds together before the run. The thresholds are commercial research conventions proposed for this wave; they are not dietary limits or independently validated materiality cutoffs.
- **Deferred inputs.** Use NOVA only in an explicitly processing-related comparison with its own availability denominator. Defer rules for new ingredient families, inferred added sugar, claims outside the locked taxonomy and ingredient-list interpretations of certification. Sampling-group labels, outcomes, brand/company and OCR metadata stay outside the control distance; they may describe groups when appropriate. A recurring H1 product profile need not coincide with a control cluster.

## Reproduction and scope of the diagnostics

Run the repository virtual-environment Python on `shared_feature_diagnostics_v1.py` from any working directory. It reads the corrected master and the three existing baseline CSVs. It outputs the inventory and upper-triangular Pearson/Spearman tables in this directory. Correlations pool regions only within the stated category and basis, except for the explicitly labelled distinct FR/UK solid-food reference. They describe redundancy and have no between-region inferential interpretation. VIF uses a common complete-case matrix for each specified feature set. Self-correlations give univariate counts; off-diagonals use pairwise complete observations. The named-peer diagnostics count assignments unless explicitly labelled distinct GTIN.

Filter the inventory by `record_kind`. `raw` separates source availability from valid observations. `joint` and `peer_retention` give set-specific denominators. `engineered` and `energy_sensitivity` describe provisional ratios. `H2_energy_only` counts candidate neighbours before matching. For `claim_comparison`, retained_n means composition/text/peer eligible from the locked layer **before** restricting to known claim states; sum the positive and documented-negative columns for the primary contrast. `cells_20_each` counts peer cells meeting the prespecified two-arm screening floor. `claim_feature` preserves the original core state counts. `H7_text_support` counts literal text hits within assessed arms. `H8_joint` measures joint evidence with nutrition. `H12_common_support` counts comparable subtype–basis cells, not products. The five lens folders link to the relevant decision rows and share this implementation.
