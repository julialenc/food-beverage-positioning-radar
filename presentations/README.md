# Presentations and business analyses

Standalone business analyses, presentation PDFs and supporting aggregate evidence.
These analyses are independent of the Streamlit application. This folder can
hold additional private-label presentations and future analyses on other topics.

regional-category medians, and on-pack positioning claims.

## Evidence and publication

Selected PDFs, aggregate evidence, documented review decisions and guides are
eligible for Git. Product-level inputs and exploratory archives remain local-only
under explicit ignore rules. They may be present on Julia's machine without
appearing in a public checkout. The four PDFs were already shared publicly; the migration and documentation
updates do not themselves stage, commit or publish this organized collection.

Counts describe observed Open Food Facts records, not sales or market share.
Retailer-sourced examples supplement qualitative analysis and are not automatically
included in numerical denominators. See the [methodology](../docs/03_PROJECT_REFERENCE/02_METHODOLOGY.md#private-label-analysis).
